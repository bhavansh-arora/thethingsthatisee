Chapter 01 — Faith

Twenty years of discovery.

Over the past twenty years, discovering Christ has transformed not just Jason’s personal life but the way he approaches photography. It’s pretty amazing how He guides the stories told through this art form.

Jason is a proud member of the St. Mary’s Catholic Church community, and has been fortunate to use his artistry to bring to life stories that capture Christ’s work in His world.

Chapter 02 — Family

A full house, a calm eye.

Life with six children means a lively reception or a toddler mid-meltdown never rattles him. Authenticity is key to every image, and he’ll answer all your questions so you feel comfortable from the first message to the final gallery.

Chapter 03 — Photo + film
