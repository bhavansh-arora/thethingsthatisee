My approach

Candid first. Gentle direction when you need it.

I photograph weddings in a candid, story-driven way. My goal is to capture honest emotion and real interactions, then step in with simple guidance for portraits so you never feel stiff or over-posed.

We’ll still set aside dedicated time for family, wedding party, and any special group photos you’d like. Once those are done, I shift back into a documentary approach so you can enjoy your people while I capture the day as it unfolds.

Catholic & Christian weddings

Respectful of the liturgy. Present for every moment.

I understand the flow of a Mass or worship service, and I know how to photograph the important moments while keeping the focus on prayer and the sacrament.

Before your wedding, I connect with your parish to learn their guidelines: where I can stand, when I can move, and whether flash is allowed. On the day, I work quietly from the sides or back as needed.

Questions

Wedding FAQ

All questions
Do you have experience with Catholic and Christian ceremonies?

Yes. I regularly photograph Catholic and Christian weddings and am very comfortable working within church guidelines. I understand the flow of a Mass or worship service and know how to photograph the important moments while keeping the focus on prayer and the sacrament.

Do you work with a second photographer?

For many weddings, yes. A second photographer is especially helpful for larger guest counts, Catholic Masses, or when you want both of you photographed getting ready in different locations.

Will we still get family and group photos if your style is candid?

Absolutely. We’ll set aside dedicated time for family, wedding-party, and any special group photos you’d like. Once those are done, I shift back into a more documentary approach.

What happens if the weather turns or the timeline changes?

Part of my job is to stay calm and adaptable. I’ll help you adjust the plan, suggest backup portrait locations (inside your church, under cover, or nearby), and make sure we still capture what matters most.

Where are you based, and do you travel?

I’m based in Centennial and photograph weddings throughout Denver, the Front Range, and across Colorado. I’m also happy to travel for out-of-state weddings, especially when you’re coming “home” to get married at a meaningful church or venue.
