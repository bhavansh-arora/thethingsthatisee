About

Hi, I’m Jason.

A happily married father of six and a jack-of-all-trades based in Centennial, Colorado. Designer, photographer, videographer, but my true passion is capturing life’s most precious moments.

My story

Whether I like it or not (spoiler: I do), Christ enters all aspects of my work.

Over the past twenty years, discovering Christ has transformed not just my personal life but the way I approach photography. It’s pretty amazing how He guides me in telling stories through this art form.

I’m a proud member of the St. Mary’s Catholic Church community, and I’ve been fortunate to use my artistry to bring stories to life that capture Christ’s work in His world.

If you’re looking for someone to make your day unforgettable through personal photography that captures who you are and the love you share, let’s talk. Being part of such an important milestone in your life would be an absolute honor.
