(04) — In the sanctuary  Catholic & Christian

I know the flow
of a Mass.

Jason regularly photographs Catholic and Christian weddings, and he’s at home working within church guidelines: present for every moment, while the focus stays on prayer and the sacrament.

  • Before the wedding, he contacts your parish about where he can stand, when he can move, and whether flash is allowed.
  • On the day he works quietly from the sides or back, respectful of the liturgy.
  • For a full Mass or a large guest list, a second photographer means nothing is missed.
