Engagements

The beginning of a journey.

An engagement shoot isn’t about posing and smiling for the camera. It’s about the unique, inspiring moments you share as a couple, and the reality of your love.

The session

Your love story, raw and real.

Whether it’s an intimate moment in a coffee shop or a playful afternoon in the park, I aim to capture the raw emotions and genuine connection between you two. Through my lens, your love story comes alive in a way that’s truly authentic.

It’s also the best rehearsal for your wedding day. You’ll get comfortable in front of the camera, we’ll get to know each other, and you’ll walk away with photos made for save-the-dates.
