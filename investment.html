    (04) — FAQ14 answers

    Good
    questions.

    Anything else? Email Jason or call 714-290-4333.

    What is your photography style?

    Jason photographs weddings in a candid, story-driven way with gentle direction when you need it. The goal is to capture honest emotion and real interactions, then step in with simple guidance for portraits so you never feel stiff or over-posed.

    Where are you based, and do you travel?

    Jason is based in Centennial and photograph weddings throughout Denver, the Front Range, and across Colorado. I’m also happy to travel for out-of-state weddings, especially when you’re coming “home” to get married at a meaningful church or venue.

    What’s included in your wedding collections?

    All wedding collections include full-resolution edited images, an online gallery to share with family and friends, and personal print rights. Many couples also add a custom wedding album (like KISSBOOKS or ZNO flatlay albums) so they have a tangible way to relive the day.

    How far in advance should we book?

    Most couples book 9 to 18 months before their wedding, especially for popular spring and fall dates. If you’re planning on a shorter timeline, feel free to reach out. Jason occasionally has last-minute availability.

    How many photos will we receive, and when?

    For a full-day wedding, you can expect a large, well-curated gallery that covers your day from start to finish. Jason sends a small preview within the first week and your full gallery within about 6 to 8 weeks, depending on the season.

    Do you have experience with Catholic and Christian ceremonies?

    Yes. Jason regularly photographs Catholic and Christian weddings and is very comfortable working within church guidelines. He understands the flow of a Mass or worship service and know how to photograph the important moments while keeping the focus on prayer and the sacrament.

    How do you handle church rules about photography?

    Before your wedding, Jason connects with your parish or church to understand their specific guidelines: where he can stand, when he can move, and whether flash is allowed. On the day, he works quietly from the sides or back as needed so he’s respectful of the liturgy while still capturing the moments that matter to you.

    Will we still get family and group photos if your style is candid?

    Absolutely. We’ll set aside dedicated time for family, wedding-party, and any special group photos you’d like. Once those are done, Jason shifts back into a more documentary approach so you can enjoy your people while he captures the day as it unfolds.

    Do you offer engagement sessions?

    Yes. Engagement sessions are a relaxed chance to get to know each other, practice being in front of the camera, and create meaningful photos on a quieter day before the wedding.

    Do you offer wedding albums and prints?

    Yes. Jason offers custom-designed wedding albums, including KISSBOOKS and ZNO flatlay books, as well as professional prints. He’ll walk you through the design process so your album tells the full story of your day in a way that feels timeless and true to you.

    Do you work with a second photographer?

    For many weddings, yes. A second photographer is especially helpful for larger guest counts, Catholic Masses, or when you want both of you photographed getting ready in different locations. They help cover alternate angles and extra candids while Jason focuses on the main story.

    Do you have backup gear?

    Yes. Jason works with professional-grade cameras, lenses, and lighting. He always brings backup gear and use multiple memory cards so your images are protected from the moment they’re taken.

    What happens if the weather is bad or the timeline changes?

    Part of the job is to stay calm and adaptable. If weather shifts or the schedule runs behind, Jason will help you adjust the plan, suggest backup portrait locations (inside your church, under cover, or nearby), and make sure we still capture what’s most important to you.

    Are your prices negotiable?

    Yes. All prices are negotiable, because Jason believes everyone deserves beautiful photography, regardless of their budget. Tell me about your day and we’ll find the right fit.
