In the sanctuary

I know the flow of a Mass.

I regularly photograph Catholic and Christian weddings, and I’m at home working within church guidelines. I capture the moments that matter while keeping the focus on prayer and the sacrament.

  • Before your wedding, I contact your parish about where I can stand, when I can move, and whether flash is allowed.
  • On the day I work quietly from the sides or back, respectful of the liturgy.
  • For a full Mass or a large guest list, a second photographer means nothing is missed.

How it works

From first hello to heirloom.

Most couples book 9 to 18 months ahead, especially for spring and fall. Shorter timeline? Ask anyway.

  1. I

    Enquire

    Tell me your date and a few details. You’ll see which collection fits straight away.

  2. II

    Talk it through

    We meet by phone or over coffee. I answer every question, and prices are always negotiable.

  3. III

    Engagement

    A relaxed session to get comfortable in front of the camera before the big day.

  4. IV

    Your day

    I coordinate with your parish beforehand, then document the day as it unfolds.

  5. V

    Your gallery

    A preview within a week, the full gallery in about 6 to 8 weeks, and an heirloom album if you’d like one.
